Privacy policy

Your training stays on your phone.

Hardset is a local-only iPhone training log. It has no account, no Hardset server, no analytics, and no advertising. This policy describes what the app does with information and what it deliberately does not do.

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Applies to Hardset 1.0 for iPhone.

In one paragraph. Hardset does not collect personal data. Your workouts, machines, gyms, notes, preferences, and body mass are stored on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a Hardset server because there is no Hardset server. The app has no account, no sync, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites. You can export a copy at any time, and deleting the app deletes the local history on it.

Who is responsible

Hardset is operated by Frankie Bisignano, an independent developer. For privacy questions, email frankabisignano@gmail.com. This is also the support address.

Training data

Hardset keeps the workouts you record on your iPhone: movements, gym and machine names, loads, repetitions, set types, workout times, and notes. It also keeps preferences such as your weight unit and rest choices. You can use the app without creating a Hardset account, because there is no account to create.

This information is stored in a local SQLite database inside the app’s container. It is not uploaded to Hardset. It is not synced to iCloud by Hardset. It is not shared with a third party unless you choose to share an export.

Backups and the device

Device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, iOS may include the app’s data in that backup under Apple’s terms. Hardset does not provide a separate backup service, and it does not sync between devices.

Because the phone is the only Hardset-held copy, an export is how you keep your history. Use Settings → Your data to prepare a CSV, JSON, or SQLite copy. JSON is the complete restorable copy; CSV restores workouts; SQLite is for keeping and inspecting and cannot be read back into Hardset.

Apple Health

Hardset 1.0 does not connect to Apple Health, does not request Health access, and does not read or write Health data. If a future version adds an optional Apple Health connection, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the connection will be off until you choose it.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional one-time Lifetime purchase. Hardset asks StoreKit on your device whether a verified, unrevoked Lifetime transaction exists, and unlocks the deep muscle map if it does. Hardset does not receive your payment-card details and does not send your purchase information to a Hardset server.

Workouts, personal history, CSV import, and every export remain available without the Lifetime purchase. The purchase does not create an account and does not unlock a subscription, because there is no subscription.

Apple keeps the purchase record under your Apple Account. Restoring a purchase and requesting a refund are handled by Apple. Hardset can only see the on-device entitlement that StoreKit reports.

Imports, exports, and sharing

A workout CSV you choose is read to prepare a preview and is saved only when you confirm the import. Import fingerprints are stored on your device to identify repeat imports. Hardset reads its own CSV and JSON exports back in; it cannot read a SQLite export back in.

Exports and workout receipts leave the app only through an action you choose, such as the share sheet. CSV contains logged training data and machine identifiers. JSON and SQLite contain the device-local tables Hardset holds. In 1.0 that does not include Apple Health data, because 1.0 does not connect to Apple Health. The recipient or app you select controls the copies you share. Deleting data in Hardset does not delete those copies.

Tracking, analytics, and third parties

Hardset contains no advertising or analytics SDK, no tracking pixels, and no third-party code that profiles you. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not use the advertising identifier.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, external fonts, or client-side scripts. It is plain HTML and CSS. Apple provides the system services Hardset uses for purchases, alarms, and device backups under Apple’s own policies.

Hardset uses Apple’s StoreKit for the Lifetime purchase, AlarmKit for rest alarms, and standard iOS storage. Apple’s handling of those system services is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Training records remain on your iPhone until you remove them. Saved workouts and sets can be deleted through History and workout receipts; an active workout can be discarded after confirmation. Archiving a gym or machine keeps its training history.

Deleting Hardset removes its local app data, including the workout database and preferences. Exports, shared receipts, Apple Health records (if any), and Apple’s purchase records are not erased by deleting Hardset. Export anything you want to keep before deletion.

You have no Hardset account to close and no server-side data to request, correct, or delete. Everything Hardset holds is on your device and is removed with the app.

Children

Hardset is a training log for adults and is not directed to children. It has no account, no social features, no advertising, and no data collection. Do not use it to record a child’s health information.

Changes

This policy is reviewed when the app’s data handling changes. A material change will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date before the version that makes the change is released. The policy will be updated before any iCloud sync, Apple Health connection, account, or server is added.
