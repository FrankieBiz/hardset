Changelog

What changed, in plain terms.

Newest release first. Each entry says what you can do now that you could not before, and anything that could affect the history already on your phone.

1.0 — first release

Hardset 1.0 is being prepared and is not yet on the App Store. It needs an iPhone running iOS 26.1 or later, in portrait.

Logging

  • Log a set with one tap on its check, or type the weight and reps on Hardset’s own keypad. The system keyboard never opens while you log.
  • Correct a saved set from the live workout or from a finished workout’s receipt. Volume, records, and progress are recalculated from the corrected figures.
  • A rest countdown that keeps its own deadline, so leaving the app does not restart it.
  • History is kept per movement and per machine. Switching machines starts a separate series, and the app never draws progress across the switch.

Numbers that say how they were made

  • Estimated one-rep maxes show their confidence. A formula’s estimate is Moderate up to 10 reps, Low at 11 and 12, and not produced above 12. Only a measured single is High.
  • The weekly Volume view counts a working set as 1.0 for each direct muscle and 0.5 for each indirect one, draws no target line, and opens the sets behind any count under “Worked from.”
  • A new record names the set it beat, and “Worked from” lists the sets that fed it and the ones left out.

Your data

  • No account, no server, and no sync. Everything is stored on the phone.
  • Export your history as CSV, JSON, or SQLite from Settings. JSON and CSV can be restored in Hardset; SQLite is a copy for keeping and inspecting, and Hardset cannot read it back.
  • Import a Strong or Hevy CSV. Imported history stays on its own series because it records no machine.

Hardset Lifetime

  • Logging, history, import, and every export are free. The optional one-time Lifetime purchase adds muscle history: when each muscle was last trained, the last four weeks against the four before, your lifetime total, and the shape of the last twelve weeks. There is no subscription.

Updating: this is the first release, so there is nothing to carry over. Because the phone holds the only copy of your history, export a JSON file before you delete the app or move to a new phone.
