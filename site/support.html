Support

One person reads this.

Hardset is made by one developer. Email frankabisignano@gmail.com and I will help. I aim to reply within two business days. If a problem will take longer, I will tell you rather than leave you waiting.

There is no phone support and no live chat. Email is the route that keeps a written record of the problem and the fix.

Before you write: include the Hardset version, your iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it. A screenshot helps. Never send an Apple Account password or payment-card details. Only send a workout export if you have decided you want to, and say what you want me to look at.

Record a workout

  1. Choose a gym if you want to identify individual machines. You can start without one and add it during a workout.
  2. Tap Start workout, then Add movement. Search the catalogue or add a custom movement when yours is not listed.
  3. Enter the weight and repetitions, then tap the check to log the set. A green logged state means the save succeeded. Numbers entered without logging are drafts and are not promised to survive closing the app.
  4. Add more sets or movements. Choose a rest duration when you want a timer.
  5. Tap Finish workout and confirm. Open History to read the saved receipt.

Correct a mistake

Tap a logged weight or repetitions to correct it, or open a movement’s options and choose Correct set. Long-press a logged set for Undo log or Edit set. Use a movement’s options to remove it. Discarding an active workout and deleting saved history require confirmation.

These actions remove data. An export is a copy from the moment you made it: restoring a JSON or CSV file brings its workouts back and leaves what is already on the phone alone. It does not merge edits you made after the export.

Change weight units

Open Settings from any main tab and select kilograms or pounds. Saved sets keep their original stored loads; changing the unit converts their display. Unitless Strong CSV weights are interpreted using the unit shown before import.

Protect your history

Hardset stores history on this iPhone only. It does not sync to another device, and there is no account or cloud copy. Deleting the app deletes local history. Install updates over the existing app; do not delete it first.

Use Settings → Your data to prepare and share a CSV, JSON, or SQLite copy. Save it somewhere you control. JSON is the complete restorable copy, including saved workouts and body mass. CSV restores workouts. SQLite is for keeping and inspecting and cannot be read back into Hardset. An export is the copy that survives a lost phone or a reinstall.

Rest alarms

Grant alarm access if you want system alerts outside the app. If access is denied, the in-app timer remains available and the banner offers Open Settings. A scheduling failure offers Retry. If saved timer state could not be recovered, Reset rest timer clears only that timer state and keeps workouts intact.

Hardset Lifetime

The optional one-time purchase unlocks muscle history. Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that bought it. A temporary product-loading failure does not mean a previous purchase has been lost. Logging, history, importing, and exporting do not require Lifetime access.

Billing, refunds, and purchase history are handled by Apple. If the purchase is not available, try again later or use Restore purchases; the free features keep working.

Apple Health

Hardset 1.0 does not connect to Apple Health. It does not request Health access and does not read or write Health data. There is nothing to connect, and there is no Health permission to revoke for this version.

Import from Strong or Hevy

Choose an English Strong or Hevy CSV under Restore or bring in workouts. Unitless Strong loads are read as the weight unit selected above, and dates without an offset use this device’s time zone. Timed, distance, and unweighted sets have no load and repetitions to record, so they are listed and left out. Workouts already imported from the same app are skipped.

Sync, devices, and platform

Hardset 1.0 is an iPhone app and requires iOS 26.1 or later. It is portrait only. It does not sync between devices, and there is no iPad or web version. A second device starts its own history; an export and restore is how you move a copy.
