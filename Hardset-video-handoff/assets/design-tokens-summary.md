# Hardset brand colors (for accurate video graphics/lower-thirds)

App is dark-mode only — do not design light-mode variants.

## Elevation (backgrounds)
- Ground (window background): `#08 0A 0E`
- Surface (cards, rows): `#15 17 1B`
- Raised (sheets, keypad): `#22 25 28`
- Overlay (system popovers): `#2F 32 36`
- Hairline / borders: `#37 39 3E`

## Text
- Primary (accent color too — the accent is white/near-white): `#F5 F5 F7`
- Secondary: `#9A 9A A4`
- Tertiary (disabled): `#7A 7D 83`

## Status
- High / good: `#34 C7 7B` (green)
- Moderate: `#E8 B9 3E` (amber)
- Low: `#E8 87 4A` (orange)

## Chart series accents (rotating palette)
- `#3C 9C D0` (blue)
- `#C1 85 03` (gold)
- `#C4 72 9F` (mauve)

in the repo, in case the dev needs to double check anything.
