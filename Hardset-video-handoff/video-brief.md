# Hardset: draft launch video brief

## Audience and idea

For lifters who already know what they want to do and want to record it quickly. Show one believable workout interaction, then the information it leaves behind. Hardset is a focused training log: fast set entry, exercise and machine-specific history, reusable plans, and transparent volume totals.

Tone: calm, direct, precise. Dark UI, measured cuts, no artificial confetti, streaks, or coaching language.

This is a draft structure, not mandatory timing. The important continuity is **plan → live set → saved history → clear summary**. The stills contain a fuller fictional training history than the single-workout recording, so use a visual transition between them; do not imply the one set in the recording produced the 24-set volume report.

## Edit notes

- The MP4 is trimmed to the actual app interaction. Retain real input moments; speed up only waits and search typing if needed. Cover the small simulator return breadcrumb at the top left.
- The numeric-pad still (`screenshots/07-numeric-pad.png`) shows an *unfilled* Chin-up set. It demonstrates the input surface only. Use the video for the completed-set claim.
- The rest-timer still (`screenshots/06-rest-timer-choice.png`) shows timer choices. It does **not** prove a background alarm fired. Treat it as optional B-roll, without a “works after force quit” claim.
- The equipment names visible in Plan and History are user-recorded names. Do not use those company logos or imply endorsement.
- Preserve the app’s dark palette and white accent. Avoid a bright neon gym look that makes the product footage feel unrelated to the graphics.
- Do not add fake numerical targets, performance scores, AI coaching, social feeds, or guaranteed muscle-growth promises.

## Alternate short cut, 15 seconds

Plan (2s) → actual load/reps/log moment (7s) → saved History (3s) → icon and “Fast, honest strength logs” (3s). Use Volume in a separate follow-up cut if the text cannot be read comfortably.

## Suggested title and post copy

**Title:** Hardset — log the work.

**Post copy:** A strength log that stays out of your way. Record a set, review the exact work, and see your training history by exercise and machine. Built for lifters who want clear numbers without the noise.
